How to Play

Welcome to Times Tables Teacher! The objective is to answer the multiplication tables questions correctly.

  1. The two numbers to be multiplied are shown.
  2. Find the product of the two numbers and enter it into the textbox then press enter.
  3. Answer the question before the time runs out.
  4. If the time runs out, the correct answer is displayed and a new question is shown.
  5. If you enter a wrong answer, the correct answer is displayed and a new question is shown.
  6. If the answer is correct, your score increases and a new question is shown.
  7. At the end, you get to view your score.
